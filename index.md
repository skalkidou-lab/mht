What’s inside

01

### Product-name categorisation

Around 110 Swedish menopausal hormone therapy product names are mapped
onto the A1–I2 category ladder: local and systemic estrogens, combined
preparations, progestogens, implants, intrauterine systems, tibolone and
testosterone.

02

### Duration correction and gap bridging

Dispensed durations are corrected for the products whose recorded
defined daily dose is unreliable, intrauterine systems are given their
real service life, and gaps of up to four weeks between consecutive
prescriptions are bridged.

03

### Approach-based exposure definitions

The `rd_approach{1,2,3,3b}_{single,multiple}` variables are written onto
a person-week skeleton, applying the `previous` and `exclude` rules and
the three-year minimum-duration rule used by the Skalkidou-lab MHT
studies.
