<p class="rw-section">What's inside</p>

<div class="rw-cards">
<div class="rw-card"><div class="rw-card-num">01</div><h3>Product-name categorisation</h3><p>The classifier ladder maps around 110 Swedish menopausal hormone therapy product names onto the A1–I2 categories. Those categories cover local and systemic estrogens, combined preparations, progestogens, implants, intrauterine systems, tibolone and testosterone.</p></div>
<div class="rw-card"><div class="rw-card-num">02</div><h3>Duration correction and gap bridging</h3><p><code>mht</code> corrects the dispensed durations of products whose recorded defined daily dose is unreliable. It gives intrauterine systems their real service life. It bridges gaps of up to four weeks between consecutive prescriptions.</p></div>
<div class="rw-card"><div class="rw-card-num">03</div><h3>Approach-based exposure definitions</h3><p><code>mht</code> writes the <code>rd_approach{1,2,3,3b}_{single,multiple}</code> variables onto a person-week skeleton. It applies the <code>previous</code> and <code>exclude</code> rules, and the three-year minimum-duration rule used by the Skalkidou-lab MHT studies.</p></div>
