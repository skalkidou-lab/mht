<p class="rw-section">What's inside</p>

<div class="rw-cards">
<div class="rw-card"><div class="rw-card-num">01</div><h3>Product-name categorisation</h3><p>Around 110 Swedish menopausal hormone therapy product names are mapped onto the A1–I2 category ladder: local and systemic estrogens, combined preparations, progestogens, implants, intrauterine systems, tibolone and testosterone.</p></div>
<div class="rw-card"><div class="rw-card-num">02</div><h3>Duration correction and gap bridging</h3><p>Dispensed durations are corrected for the products whose recorded defined daily dose is unreliable, intrauterine systems are given their real service life, and gaps of up to four weeks between consecutive prescriptions are bridged.</p></div>
<div class="rw-card"><div class="rw-card-num">03</div><h3>Approach-based exposure definitions</h3><p>The <code>rd_approach{1,2,3,3b}_{single,multiple}</code> variables are written onto a person-week skeleton, applying the <code>previous</code> and <code>exclude</code> rules and the three-year minimum-duration rule used by the Skalkidou-lab MHT studies.</p></div>
</div>
