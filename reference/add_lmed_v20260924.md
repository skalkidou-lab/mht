# Add 2026-09-24 MHT exposure to a person-week skeleton

Writes every column that \`add_lmed_v20260922()\` writes, and adds
\`approach4\`, \`rd_approach4_single\` and \`rd_approach4_multiple\`. It
reads \`inst/2023-mht/product_table_20260924.xlsx\` and
\`inst/2023-mht/dataDictionary20260924.xlsx\`, and no other workbook.

## Usage

``` r
add_lmed_v20260924(
  skeleton,
  lmed,
  id_name = "lopnr",
  create_rd = TRUE,
  verbose = TRUE
)
```

## Arguments

- skeleton:

  A person-week \`data.table\` with \`id\` and \`isoyearweek\`. Weekly
  rows only, as for \`add_lmed_v20260922()\`.

- lmed:

  A \`data.table\` of dispensed prescriptions.

- id_name:

  The person identifier column of \`lmed\`.

- create_rd:

  Logical. If \`FALSE\`, writes no \`rd_approach\*\` column.

- verbose:

  Logical. If \`TRUE\`, report progress with \`message()\`.

## Value

\`invisible(skeleton)\`. Mutation is in place.

## Details

Every column other than the three approach 4 columns equals the output
of \`add_lmed_v20260922()\`. Approach 4 splits systemic oestrogen by
route and by progestogen type. The \`post_grouping\` sheet gives it
these levels:

- \`oral_estrogen_progesterone\`: B4 or B11, or oral oestrogen (A2 or
  A6) with C1 or C5.

- \`oral_estrogen_other_progestogen\`: B2, B3, B5, B6, B8, B9, B10 or
  B12, or A2 or A6 with C3, C4, D1, D2, D3, E1, I1 or I2.

- \`transdermal_estrogen_progesterone\`: transdermal oestrogen (A1) with
  C1 or C5.

- \`transdermal_estrogen_other_progestogen\`: B1 or B7, or A1 with C3,
  C4, D1, D2, D3, E1, I1 or I2.

- \`estrogen_only\`: A1, A2 or A6, in a week with no B1 to B12, C1 to
  C5, D1 to D3, E1, I1 or I2.

- \`tibolone\`: F1.

- \`local_or_none_mht\`: a week that lights no level above.

The resolver is that of \`add_lmed_v20260922()\`. When two levels are
lit, \`approach4\` reports the level that started most recently. Two
levels that start in the same week are \`clashingprescriptions\` until
the treated episode ends.

## See also

[`add_lmed_v20260922`](https://skalkidou-lab.github.io/mht/reference/add_lmed_v20260922.md),
which documents the behaviour the two entry points share.
\`vignette("lmed-v20260924")\` explains approach 4, and checks each of
its worked cases when it builds.
