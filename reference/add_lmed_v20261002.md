# Add 2026-10-02 MHT exposure to a person-week skeleton

Writes every column that \`add_lmed_v20260924()\` writes, and adds
\`approach5\`, \`rd_approach5_single\` and \`rd_approach5_multiple\`. It
reads \`inst/2023-mht/product_table_20261002.xlsx\` and
\`inst/2023-mht/dataDictionary20261002.xlsx\`, and no other workbook.

## Usage

``` r
add_lmed_v20261002(
  skeleton,
  lmed,
  id_name = "lopnr",
  create_rd = TRUE,
  verbose = TRUE
)
```

## Arguments

- skeleton:

  A person-week \`data.table\` with \`id\` and \`isoyearweek\`. Weekly
  rows only, as for \`add_lmed_v20260924()\`.

- lmed:

  A \`data.table\` of dispensed prescriptions.

- id_name:

  The person identifier column of \`lmed\`.

- create_rd:

  Logical. If \`FALSE\`, writes no \`rd_approach\*\` column.

- verbose:

  Logical. If \`TRUE\`, report progress with \`message()\`.

## Value

\`invisible(skeleton)\`. Mutation is in place.

## Details

Every column other than the three approach 5 columns equals the output
of \`add_lmed_v20260924()\`. Approach 5 pools the oestrogen routes of
approach 4, and gives the hormonal IUD (E1) its own level. The
\`post_grouping\` sheet gives it the levels below, where oestrogen means
A1, A2 or A6:

- \`estrogen_progesterone\`: B4 or B11, or oestrogen with C1 or C5.

- \`estrogen_other_progestogen\`: B1, B2, B3, B5 to B10, or B12, or
  oestrogen with C3, C4, D1, D2, D3, I1 or I2.

- \`estrogen_iud\`: oestrogen with E1.

- \`estrogen_only\`: oestrogen, in a week with no B1 to B12, C1 to C5,
  D1 to D3, E1, I1 or I2.

- \`tibolone\`: F1.

- \`local_or_none_mht\`: a week that lights no level above.

A week with oestrogen, E1 and one of C3, C4, D1, D2, D3, I1 or I2 lights
both \`estrogen_iud\` and \`estrogen_other_progestogen\`.

The resolver is that of \`add_lmed_v20260924()\`. When two levels are
lit, \`approach5\` reports the level that started most recently. Two
levels that start in the same week are \`clashingprescriptions\` until
the treated episode ends.

## See also

[`add_lmed_v20260924`](https://skalkidou-lab.github.io/mht/reference/add_lmed_v20260924.md),
which documents the behaviour the two entry points share.
\`vignette("lmed-v20261002")\` explains approaches 1 to 5.
