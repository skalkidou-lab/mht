# mht: Menopausal hormone therapy exposure definitions for Swedish registry data

Derives menopausal hormone therapy (MHT) exposure from Swedish
prescription registry (LMED) data. Categorises dispensed product names
into MHT groups, corrects dispensed durations, bridges gaps between
consecutive prescriptions, and applies the approach-based exposure
definitions used by the Skalkidou-lab MHT studies to person-week
skeletons.
