# mht: Menopausal hormone therapy exposure definitions for Swedish registry data

Derives menopausal hormone therapy (MHT) exposure from Swedish
prescription registry (LMED) data. \`mht\` categorises dispensed product
names into MHT groups. It corrects dispensed durations. It bridges gaps
between consecutive prescriptions. It then applies the approach-based
exposure definitions used by the Skalkidou-lab MHT studies to
person-week skeletons.
